# Romantic Date Proposal Web App 🌸💌

A delightful, interactive web application inspired by the viral date invitation video.

---

## ✨ Features
1. **Playful "No" Dodge Physics**:
   - The "No" button mischievously dodges away whenever her cursor or touch gets close.
   - Dodge counter ("1 dodge", "2 dodges", ...).
   - Humorous phrases update on each dodge ("Nice try — it dodged you", "No is wilting...", "Yes is blooming, hm? 🌸").
   - The "YES" button grows larger and more inviting with every dodge attempt!
2. **Layered Flower Bloom Graphic**:
   - Gentle breathing animation with translucent rose petals and leaf.
3. **Sound Effects & Confetti**:
   - Built-in Web Audio API sound synthesis (no external audio files needed; plays crisp pop, dodge whoosh, and celebratory harp chimes).
   - Confetti burst on "YES" and floating romantic hearts on the letter screen.
4. **Interactive Date, Time & Plan Picker**:
   - Date picker (auto-defaults to the upcoming weekend).
   - Time picker (e.g., 7:30 PM).
   - Plan badges (☕ Coffee, 🎬 Movie, 🍝 Dinner, 🌳 Walk in the park, 🍸 Drinks, 🎁 Surprise me).
5. **The Romantic Sealed Letter**:
   - Personalized letter with your names, date, time, and chosen plan.
   - Direct **"Send confirmation on WhatsApp"** button: pre-fills a romantic confirmation message ready to send with one tap!
6. **Instant Customization & Shareable Links**:
   - Click the **"⚙️ Customize"** button on top-right to enter her name, your name, and your WhatsApp phone number.
   - Click **"Copy Shareable Link"** to generate a direct personalized link (e.g., `?to=Maya&from=Chethiya&phone=+9477...`).

---

Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser, or run:
```bash
open index.html
```
